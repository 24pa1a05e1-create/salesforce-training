# What is CRM ? 
CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management. It is a system used by organizations to manage customer information, communication, sales, and services in one place. CRM helps companies improve customer relationships and business growth.

# Why Companies Use Salesforce ?
Companies use Salesforce because it is a cloud-based CRM platform that helps manage customer data, automate business processes, track sales, and improve communication. It increases productivity and helps organizations provide better customer service.

## Business Flow  
Lead-->Contact-->Oppurtunity-->Customer  
### Lead:
This is the starting point. A person or business who has shown some interest in what your'e selling.They are "potential" but unverified.  
### Contact:
At this stage ,you reach out to the lead.your'e introducing yourself,asking questions,and determining if your product actually meet their needs.  
### Oppurtunity:
This is the "breakthrough" moment.the contact has confirmed they have a problem you can solve and the budget to pay for it.you are now actively negotiating or providing a proposal.
### Customer:
Success!The deal is closed,the contract is signed,and the individual has officially purchased your product or service.

# Key Concept

## Acount
An Account in Salesforce represents an organization, company, or institution with which a business has a relationship. In the college admission process, the college itself can be considered as an Account.

## Contact

A Contact represents an individual person associated with an Account. In college admission, a student applying to the college is considered a Contact.

## Lead

A Lead is a person who shows interest in a product or service but has not yet become a confirmed customer. In the college admission process, a student making an admission inquiry is considered a Lead.

## Opportunity

An Opportunity represents a possible business deal or successful conversion. In college admission, the student admission process after showing interest can be considered an Opportunity.

# Real World Mapping(College Admission)
## Account:college/university
Represents the Educational institution managing admissions and student records.

## Contact:Student/Parent
Stores personal details such as name,phn number,email,and address of student/guardian.

## Lead:Interested Student
A student who has shown interest in joining the college through admission calls/counselling.

## Oppurtunity:Admission Application/Enrollment
Represents the chance of converting interested student in to a successfully admitted student.
